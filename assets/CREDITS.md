# Earth map credits

Local image data are copied without image modification from three.js r160 examples, with base64 encoding only for repository transport.

- [Day map: earth_atmos_2048.jpg](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r160/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg)
- [Night map: earth_lights_2048.png](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r160/examples/textures/planets/earth_lights_2048.png)
- [Cloud map: earth_clouds_1024.png](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r160/examples/textures/planets/earth_clouds_1024.png)

Earth imagery reference: [NASA Earth Observatory — Blue Marble 2002](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/), NASA GSFC, Reto Stöckli and Robert Simmon; see original credits for additional contributors. Three.js example assets are the immediate source; original acquisition dates and exact processing for individual example maps were not independently established. Night and cloud layers are historical illustrative composites, not live observations. No affiliation or endorsement by NASA or three.js is implied.
